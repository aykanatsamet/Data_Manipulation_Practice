
# Model Results – Customer Segmentation

## Methodology
Customer segmentation was performed using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) features
derived from transactional data. The features were scaled and clustered using the
K-Means algorithm.

## Model Selection
- K-Means clustering was selected due to its interpretability and efficiency.
- The optimal number of clusters was determined using the Elbow Method
  and Silhouette Score analysis.

## Results Summary
- The final model identified distinct customer segments with clearly
  differentiated purchasing behaviors.
- High-value customers demonstrated high frequency and monetary value.
- At-risk customers showed increased recency and declining engagement.

## Business Value
The resulting segments enable:
- Personalized marketing strategies
- Improved customer retention planning
- Data-driven resource allocation

## Notes
This model is unsupervised and exploratory by nature.
Results should be periodically updated with new transactional data.
