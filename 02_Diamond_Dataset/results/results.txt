DIAMOND PRICE PREDICTION - MODEL RESULTS

1. Model Comparison:
--------------------
- Linear Regression (Baseline):
  * R2 Score: ~0.88
  * MAE: ~800 $
  * RMSE: ~1300 $

- Polynomial Regression (Degree 2):
  * R2 Score: ~0.96 
  * MAE: ~400 $
  * RMSE: ~800 $

2. Key Insights:
----------------
- Moving from Linear to Polynomial regression (Degree 2) reduced the error by nearly 50%.
- The relationship between Carat and Price is non-linear, which is why the Polynomial model performed better.
- Data cleaning (removing 0 values in X, Y, Z and filtering extreme outliers) was crucial for model stability.
- Ordinal encoding for Cut, Color, and Clarity helped the model understand quality rankings.
